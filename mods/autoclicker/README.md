# Simple AutoClicker for Horizon

A native [Horizon](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon) mod (Minecraft 26.3): a port of
[Simple AutoClicker](https://github.com/johnnyscriptt/simpleautoclicker) (MIT). `,` toggles left-click
autoclicking and `.` right-click, each at its own interval (Options > Mods > Simple AutoClicker: 50 ms to
10 s, 200 ms by default); two more keys (unbound by default) hold a button down instead. Clicks go through
the game's own attack and use, only while playing with no screen open, and stop when the player leaves the
world. The action bar says each toggle.
